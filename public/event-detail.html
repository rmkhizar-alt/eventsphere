CodeForge 2026: 36-Hour National Hackathon.

Sep 15, 2026 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM Advanced Computing Center Dept. of Computer Science

About this event

CodeForge is the flagship 36-hour national hackathon of the Department of Computer Science. Teams of two to four students race to prototype working solutions across three tracks — Artificial Intelligence, Web3, and Internet of Things — with mentors from leading tech firms roaming the floor all night.

Registration includes meals, snacks, swag kits and unlimited coffee. Shortlisted teams pitch to an industry panel on Sunday evening; the top three teams take home cash prizes plus direct interview fast-tracks with sponsor companies.
