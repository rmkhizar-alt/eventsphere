Accounts & Registration

How do I create an account?

Open the Sign Up page and enter your name, campus email, department and enrolment number, then set a password. Validation checks run as you type — a valid email format is required before the account can be created.

Can visitors browse without logging in?

Yes — events, galleries, reviews, FAQs and contact pages are fully open. Only actions like registering, waitlisting, feedback and certificate downloads need an account, and the site will prompt you to log in when you try one.

I forgot my password. What now?

Use the "Forgot password?" link on the login page — a reset link goes to your registered campus email. Admins can also reset or suspend accounts from their dashboard.

Events & Seats

How do live seat counts work?

Every event has a configurable capacity set by its organizer. The seat bar on each card updates instantly as people register or cancel — when it hits zero, registration closes and the waitlist activates automatically.

How does the waitlist promotion work?

When a confirmed participant cancels before the cutoff, the first person on the waitlist is auto-promoted to confirmed and receives a notification with their QR pass — no manual intervention needed.

Can I cancel my registration?

Yes, any time before the event's cancellation cutoff from your dashboard. Your slot releases instantly and feeds the waitlist queue.

Certificates & Payments

How do I get my e-certificate?

Attendance is marked when an organizer scans your QR pass at the venue. After the event, eligible participants see a "Download Certificate" option on their dashboard once the certificate fee is recorded.

Is online payment integrated?

The system records certificate fee details for participants, but actual payment processing is intentionally out of scope for this build — no money moves through EventSphere.
