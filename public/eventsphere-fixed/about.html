Why we exist

From missed posters to a living calendar.

Colleges run dozens of events every semester — hackathons, cultural nights, sports meets, workshops. Yet details traditionally travel by noticeboard and word-of-mouth, so students miss updates, organizers juggle spreadsheets, and participation stays lower than it should be.

EventSphere centralizes everything: real-time event discovery with live seat counts, online registration with QR check-in, automated waitlists, e-certificates, media galleries, and role-based dashboards for participants, organizers, and admins.

How EventSphere Works

One flow, four roles — from proposal to certificate.

STEP 01

Organizer Proposes

Staff create the event with venue, capacity, and rulebook — it enters a pending approval queue.

STEP 02

Admin Approves

Admins review proposals for quality and clashes, then publish them live to the whole campus.

STEP 03

Students Register

Participants grab seats in real time (or join the waitlist) and receive QR passes for entry.

STEP 04

Certificates Issue

QR attendance feeds certificates and feedback — closing the loop for everyone involved.
