Support

Questions, Answered.

How do I register for an event?

Create a student account with your name, email, department and enrolment number, then browse Live Events and hit Register Now. Seat counts update in real time — if an event is full you automatically join the priority waitlist.

Can I cancel my registration?

Yes — registrations can be cancelled from your dashboard any time before the event's cutoff date. Your slot is released instantly and the first waitlisted student is auto-promoted.

How do I get my e-certificate?

Attendees are marked present via QR check-in on event day. After the event, pay the certificate fee through your profile to unlock and download your verified e-certificate.

Is online payment integrated?

The system records certificate fee details for participants, but live payment processing is beyond the scope of this project build.
